# Name and logo

The code in this repository is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). You can use it, change it, and share your changes, including for commercial use.

The license covers the code, not the name. Section 6 of the Apache License says it does not grant permission to use the licensor's trade names or marks. "City Print" and the City Print logo (the amber map tile) belong to Aurora Tejeda.

## If you share a modified version

- Give it a different name and icon. Don't call it "City Print" or anything easily confused with it.
- You may say it is "based on City Print" or "a fork of City Print" to describe where it came from.
- Keep the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files, and note that you changed the files you changed (Apache License, section 4).

## What's fine without asking

- Building and running City Print unmodified, and sharing unmodified copies under its own name.
- Mentioning City Print by name in articles, videos, reviews and tutorials.
- Sharing maps and posters you make with City Print. They are yours.
